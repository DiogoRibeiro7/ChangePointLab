# Code of Conduct

ChangePointLab is an open-source research and engineering project. Contributors
are expected to keep discussions respectful, specific, and focused on improving
the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be clear and constructive when reporting issues or reviewing changes.
- Critique code, documentation, evidence, and methodology rather than people.
- Respect different experience levels and research backgrounds.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when they affect technical recommendations.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions after maintainers redirect them.
- Misrepresenting unsupported scientific claims as validated project results.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may limit participation when
behavior harms the project. Security reports should use private vulnerability
reporting rather than public discussion.
